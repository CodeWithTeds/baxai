1. ADMINISTRATOR ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP DIAGRAM (ADMIN ERD)
   Subsystem Scope: System Governance, Catalog Configuration, and Financial Control Subsystem

The Administrator Entity-Relationship Diagram (Admin ERD) defines the structural backbone for organizational governance, multi-tiered access control, operational thresholds, and fiscal auditability within the printing management platform. At the root of this subsystem is the USERS entity, which implements granular Role-Based Access Control (role, permissions JSON matrix) and Two-Factor Authentication (two_factor_enabled) to govern system access across superadmins, store managers, and operational auditors. This administrative core establishes a direct one-to-many relationship with STAFF_PROFILES to manage departmental personnel, TASKS to issue operational directives and shift deadlines, and AUDIT_LOGS to maintain an immutable audit trail capturing actor IDs, mutation payloads (old_values, new_values), and client network telemetry. Parallel to personnel management, the Admin ERD orchestrates the commercial catalog and customizer parameters through hierarchical relationships: PRODUCT_CATEGORIES classifies base products (PRODUCTS), which fork into discrete physical permutations (PRODUCT_VARIANTS) and parametric design rules (CUSTOMIZATION_TEMPLATES). The CUSTOMIZATION_TEMPLATES entity encapsulates spatial bounding zones, permitted typography, and approved CMYK/RGB color palettes required to restrict client-side WebGL canvas inputs within printable physical thresholds. Operationally, the schema bridges product definitions with raw materials through PRINT_CATEGORIES, PRINT_ITEMS, and INVENTORY_MATERIALS. A vital automated feedback loop is maintained via MATERIAL_STOCK_LOGS, tracking inbound replenishment, consumption deltas, and scrap rates against predefined safety stock thresholds (reorder_level). Finally, the administrative fiscal domain governs customer incentive engines (VIP_TIERS, REWARDS, and DISCOUNTS) and monitors transaction lifecycles through ORDERS, ORDER_ITEMS, and PAYMENTS, culminating in the automatic generation of BIR-compliant INVOICES that segregate net vatable sales and 12% statutory Value-Added Tax.


2. STAFF ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP DIAGRAM (STAFF ERD)
   Subsystem Scope: Prepress Proofing, Production Job Routing, and Quality Assurance Subsystem

The Staff Entity-Relationship Diagram (Staff ERD) models a top-down, vertical manufacturing execution pipeline specifically engineered to eliminate prepress human errors, route machine workloads efficiently, and enforce strict quality verification standards. Anchored by the authenticated shop worker (USERS linked to STAFF_PROFILES), this subsystem consumes verified client purchase specifications from ORDER_ITEMS and merges them with spatial coordinate matrices stored in ORDER_CUSTOMIZATIONS (capturing print locations, method classifications like DTF, UV Flatbed, or Sublimation, and 3D affine transformation vectors including scale, rotation, and translation offsets). Before physical printing commences, the workflow enforces a digital quality gate via the PROOF_APPROVALS entity, which associates a designated prepress graphic artist with an order item. This table manages digital proof revisions (proof_version, proof_pdf_url) and establishes a bidirectional communication channel supported by PROOF_REVISION_MESSAGES to capture iterative client feedback, visual markup attachments, and design modifications. Once customer authorization is secured, the state transition unlocks the manufacturing layer through PRODUCTION_JOBS, which assigns unique job tickets (job_ticket_number) to active hardware nodes (MACHINE_STATIONS). This ensures optimized queue routing across Roland UV, Mimaki flatbed, and DTF heat transfer stations. During print execution, the job ticket references physical substrates (PRINT_ITEMS) and triggers programmatic deductions in INVENTORY_MATERIALS for consumables such as meters of transfer film and milliliters of ink. The pipeline terminates at the QC_INSPECTION_LOGS entity, where a certified Quality Control inspector conducts formal multi-point evaluations—specifically verifying Delta-E color tolerances (color_accuracy_passed), print bleed alignment (alignment_passed), and heat cure adhesion (surface_finish_passed)—guaranteeing that only verified defect-free goods transition to final packaging and dispatch.


3. CUSTOMER ENTITY-RELATIONSHIP DIAGRAM (CUSTOMER ERD)
   Subsystem Scope: Storefront, 3D WebGL Customizer, and Customer Relationship Subsystem

The Customer Entity-Relationship Diagram (Customer ERD) formalizes the client-facing transaction lifecycle, bridging interactive WebGL design authoring with shopping cart management, multi-gateway fiscal settlement, and customer retention programs. The domain is centered around the CUSTOMERS entity, which persists individual, corporate, and walk-in client records along with lifetime expenditure totals and real-time loyalty point balances. Customers maintain multiple delivery destinations via CUSTOMER_ADDRESSES and are dynamically stratified into promotional classifications through VIP_TIERS. This tiering system controls reward redemption multipliers and unlocks exclusive claims cataloged in REWARDS, with customer-specific claims recorded in CUSTOMER_REWARDS to produce usable checkout discount tokens. The interactive purchasing workflow commences when customers browse the categorized catalog (PRODUCT_CATEGORIES to PRODUCTS and PRODUCT_VARIANTS) and select customizer-ready blanks configured with 3D model assets (model_3d_url, viewer_type). Upon adding items to the checkout manifest, an entry is instantiated in ORDERS containing comprehensive transaction totals (subtotals, customization add-ons, shipping fees, applied coupon deductions, and structured milestone tracking arrays in JSON format). Each ordered unit is captured within ORDER_ITEMS and supplemented by ORDER_CUSTOMIZATIONS, which records the client's high-resolution raster or vector artwork URLs (processed at 300 DPI) alongside the Three.js viewport transformation matrices (transform_matrix). To safeguard customer intent, custom order items automatically spawn entries in PROOF_APPROVALS, presenting an interactive review window where the client can either execute a 1-click approval or log revision annotations. Once validated, the financial settlement is captured through PAYMENTS, which logs electronic payment webhooks and confirmation reference tokens from regional Philippine payment gateways (GCash, Maya, Debit/Credit Card, and COD), thereby locking the order and feeding it directly into the fulfillment pipeline.


4. DETAILED DATA FLOW DIAGRAM (DDFD) & PROCESS SIMULATION
   Subsystem Scope: Level 0 Context and Level 1 Multi-Actor Process Simulation (Gane & Sarson Notation)

The Detailed Data Flow Diagram (DDFD) models the dynamic information architecture, external actor interfaces, inter-process communication channels, and data repository transactions that govern the automated printing ecosystem under Gane & Sarson notation. At the macro context level (Process 0.0), the diagram establishes formal input/output contracts connecting seven disparate external entities—namely the Customer, Shop Administrator, Print Staff, AI Vision & RIP Engine, Payment Gateways (GCash/Maya/Cards), Material Suppliers, and Montalban Logistics Couriers. 

Decomposed into its Level 1 architectural subsystems, Process 1.0 (Customer Storefront & 3D Customizer) interfaces with Process 2.0 (AI Prepress & Digital Proofing) by extracting 3D GLB model specifications and base pricing rules from Data Store D2 (Product Catalog & 3D Models) and persisting affine transformation matrices into Data Store D3 (3D Customizations & Vector Assets). Process 2.0 establishes an asynchronous pipeline with the AI Vision & RIP Engine, which ingests raw client artwork, executes deep-learning super-resolution upscaling to 300 DPI, synthesizes bleed boundary lines, and validates CMYK color space profiles. These enhanced vector assets are saved to D3, while generated watermarked proofs are stored in Data Store D4 (Digital Proofs & Revisions) to facilitate an interactive approval and revision loop with the Customer and Prepress Staff.

Upon client proof authorization, execution transitions to Process 3.0 (Multi-Gateway Billing & VIP System), which queries Data Store D6 (Payments, Invoices & VIP Loyalty) to evaluate tier discounts, invokes payment gateway APIs for instant transaction settlement, records confirmed orders in Data Store D5 (Orders, Line Items & Status Tracking), issues official BIR-compliant VAT e-invoices, and dispatches electronic receipts with live tracking metadata to the buyer. This verified state triggers Process 4.0 (Production Dispatch & QC System), which pulls pending production jobs from D5 and compiles machine-specific job tickets into Data Store D7 (Production Jobs & Machine Queues), routing payloads dynamically to Roland UV flatbed, DTF transfer, or sublimation lines. As printing proceeds, consumption telemetry is transmitted directly to Process 5.0 (Stores Inventory & Procurement), which continuously decrements physical consumable balances in Data Store D8 (Raw Inventory & Supplier POs) and compares on-hand levels against minimum safety thresholds. Should stock deplete past safety buffers, Process 5.0 generates automated reorder alerts to the Administrator, prepares authorized supplier Purchase Orders (POs) transmitted to Material Vendors, and reconciles inbound supplier deliveries into D8. 

Finally, completed physical outputs undergo rigorous multi-point verification (Delta-E color tolerances, bleed alignment, and curing adhesion) logged into D7, after which Process 4.0 updates D5 milestone tracking to mark parcels ready for courier handover, concluding with localized delivery and digital proof-of-delivery (POD) sign-off to the Montalban client.

## 3. Data Dictionary: Inflows & Outflows

| Entity / Actor | Data Inflow (Into Central System) | Data Outflow (From Central System) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **👤 Customer** | • 3D substrate & variant selection<br/>• Custom uploaded logos, texts & transforms<br/>• Digital proof approval / revision notes<br/>• Checkout payment authorization & shipping info | • Interactive 3D WebGL preview & swatches<br/>• Watermarked digital proof PDF v1.0/v2.0<br/>• Official e-receipt, VAT invoice & tracking URL<br/>• Real-time SMS/Email order status alerts |
| **👑 Shop Administrator** | • Product catalog, base rates & 3D GLB models<br/>• Tier pricing formulas & volume discount rules<br/>• Staff workstation allocation & shift tasks<br/>• Supplier purchase order (PO) authorizations | • Gross revenue, net profit & BIR VAT reports<br/>• Real-time low-stock safety threshold alerts<br/>• Machine throughput, uptime & scrap waste metrics<br/>• Staff performance KPIs & QC audit trail |
| **🛠️ Print Staff / Operator** | • Prepress proof validation & layout tweaks<br/>• Machine job status updates (Roland UV/DTF/Sublimation)<br/>• Material consumption telemetry (meters/ml/blanks)<br/>• Multi-point QC checklist submission (Delta-E, bleed) | • Approved 300 DPI vector assets & RIP print profiles<br/>• Prioritized station job queue & work tickets<br/>• Daily shift task directives & machine assignments<br/>• Packing manifest & shipping dispatch labels |
| **🤖 AI Vision & RIP Engine** | • 300 DPI super-resolution vectorized assets<br/>• Automated bleed margins & contour cutting lines<br/>• Resolution score & color space (CMYK) validation | • Raw uploaded customer raster images<br/>• Printable bounding box coordinates & product DPI specs |
| **💳 Payment Gateway** | • Webhook payment settlement verification<br/>• Gateway transaction reference number & auth code<br/>• Instant charge capture acknowledgment | • Encrypted payment charge payload<br/>• Order reference ID & gross settlement amount |
| **🏭 Material Suppliers** | • Inbound stock delivery manifest & batch invoices<br/>• Substrate & ink unit cost catalog updates<br/>• Raw material availability notices | • Authorized material purchase orders (POs)<br/>• Delivery delivery time specifications |
| **🚚 Montalban Courier** | • Courier pickup confirmation & rider dispatch notice<br/>• Waybill tracking number & milestone updates<br/>• Final delivery proof of delivery (POD) | • Ready parcel manifest & customer shipping address<br/>• Parcel package dimensions & weight specifications |
